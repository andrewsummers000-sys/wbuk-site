Frequently asked questions about WBUK

What does WBUK do?

WBUK is a UK hospitality consultancy specialising in procurement and operational improvement and efficiency. We help hotels, restaurants, gastro pubs, care homes, private hospitals and catering companies reduce costs, strengthen suppliers and run more profitably.

How long has WBUK been trading?

WBUK was established in 2000. We work with hospitality businesses across the whole of the United Kingdom.

Which businesses does WBUK work with?

We work with hotels, restaurants, gastro pubs, aged care providers, private hospitals and catering companies, from single-site independents to multi-site operators.

How can a hospitality consultancy reduce my costs?

We review what you buy and how you operate, identify where money is being lost, and put in place better sourcing, supplier terms and processes. The aim is lower cost and better profit without lowering the standards your guests and residents expect.

What is included in procurement consultancy for hospitality?

Our procurement services cover spend optimisation, tender management, supplier development and supplier risk review, plus technology sourcing. We are independent of suppliers, so our advice is based on what is right for your business.

What does operational improvement and efficiency mean?

It means getting the business running better: analysing how work is done, improving processes, removing waste, strengthening management reporting and increasing profit. Our Operational Support service is delivered like a management consultancy engagement, tailored to hospitality.

Can WBUK help with tendering and supplier contracts?

Yes. Our Tender Management service supports you from defining requirements and approaching the market through to evaluation and contract award, and Supplier Development helps you manage the relationships and risks afterwards.
