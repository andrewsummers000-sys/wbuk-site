Procurement services: your questions answered

What are hospitality procurement services?

Hospitality procurement services help hotels and other hospitality businesses buy better: reviewing spend, setting category strategies, tendering to the market, choosing suppliers and managing them over time to reduce costs and risk.

What is spend optimisation?

Spend optimisation is a structured review of what a business buys and from whom, to find savings, remove duplicate or unnecessary spend and agree better terms with suppliers.

What is tender management?

Tender management is running a competitive process to select suppliers: defining requirements, approaching the market, evaluating responses fairly and supporting negotiation through to contract award.

What is supplier development?

Supplier development covers supplier risk review, continuity planning and relationship management, so that the suppliers your business depends on perform well and reliably.

Does WBUK only work with hotels?

No. We also work with restaurants, gastro pubs, aged care providers, private hospitals and catering companies across the UK.
